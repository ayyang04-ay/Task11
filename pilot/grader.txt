You are grading an independent read on a client's small-molecule MGAT1 programme in STK11-mutant NSCLC: whether to advance toward IND-enabling work, and the study package required to get there. The responder had a working directory containing in vivo and in vitro screen counts, knockout glycoproteomics, compound registry and biophysical tables, assay and phenotype figure records, five crystal structures, and a methods document. A result counts only where the value is right and the basis given produces it.

A) MUST BE PRESENT AND CORRECT
The following are the key requirements of a correct answer. Use your judgment to identify any additional required content not listed here.
- States that no compound in the package has been tested in any cell, and treats that as the gating gap.
- Establishes that the phenotype depends on MGAT1 catalytic activity, citing the wild-type versus catalytically-dead cDNA rescue.
- Reports the genotype-selectivity of MGAT1 depletion using a comparison corrected for differential guide representation between the two backgrounds, and indicates whether that conclusion is stable to the choice of correction.
- Characterises the knockout glycoproteomics quantitatively and establishes how many complex or sialylated sites were actually quantified before drawing any product-side conclusion.
- Concludes that neither acquired compound is validated chemical matter, with a basis that produces that conclusion.
- Proposes a pharmacodynamic assay that does not rest on the existing glycoproteomics as a product-side reference.
- Proposes selectivity work scoped to the relevant paralogs, and takes a safety position rather than only noting the absence of safety data.
- Establishes cross-species potency before proposing any mouse in vivo efficacy study, or states why that study is uninterpretable without it.
- Gives, for every number, the file it came from and how it was reduced.

B) PENALIZE FOR
The following are common errors observed on this task. This list is not exhaustive - apply your judgment to any other issues you identify.
- Normalising the quantitative channels to equal total or median intensity, or reporting a depletion of complex glycans that follows from having done so.
- Reporting high-mannose species as decreasing in the knockout.
- Drawing a conclusion about product-side depletion without establishing how many such sites were quantified.
- Treating a surface lectin signal ratio and a whole-lysate glycopeptide intensity ratio as directly comparable magnitudes.
- Reading the two-fold dissociation-constant shifts in the competition experiments as cooperativity, where the records state each experiment used a freshly prepared surface at a different immobilisation level.
- Comparing guide depletion across the two backgrounds without correcting for differential representation, or asserting the selectivity magnitude without testing whether it survives a different correction.
- Quoting a fitted potency for a compound whose dose-response does not approach full inhibition.
- Proposing a mouse in vivo efficacy study without establishing that the compounds inhibit mouse MGAT1.
- Treating the absence of toxicology data as grounds for making no safety assessment.
- Reporting a value whose stated derivation does not reproduce it.

C) NEUTRAL
The following are examples of acceptable variations that should not affect the score. Other variations may also be neutral - use your judgment.
- Any defensible glycan classification scheme, and percentages within a few points of those in the reference.
- Control-guide normalisation or any other method that demonstrably corrects for differential representation.
- Excluding the acquired compounds on orthogonal binding evidence, on incomplete inhibition, or on both.
- Nominating either the most potent analogue or the one carrying the mechanistic package as development candidate, where justified.
- Any of several study designs, sequencings or timelines for the proposed work.
- Whether the safety risk is called a blocker or a characterisable risk.
- Whether the asset is positioned on STK11-mutant disease or repositioned more broadly, where the positioning follows from the selectivity analysis presented.
- Order, layout, length, headings and units.
